What this app is

Anvil helps you break a year into 12-week execution cycles, track daily habits, and optionally link with one accountability partner.

Data we collect

Stored only on your device (never sent to our servers unless you use the optional Partner feature below): - Your name, 10-year vision, 3-year milestone, and 12-week goal text - Your daily tasks and completion history - Your streaks and progress metrics

Sent to our servers, only if you choose to use the Accountability Partner feature: - The email address of anyone you invite - Only the specific fields you've explicitly chosen to share with your partner (see "What your partner can see" in the app's Partner settings) — anything you haven't opted into sharing is never transmitted to our servers in shareable form for your partner to read.

We do not sell your data. We do not use your data for advertising.

Accountability partners and shared data

If you link with a partner: - You control exactly what they can see, field by field, at any time. - This is enforced on our server — fields you haven't opted to share are not included in what your partner's app receives, not merely hidden in their app's display. - If you delete your account or your data, everything your partner could see from you is removed immediately, in the same action. This is automatic and cannot be undone. - If you unlink from a partner, the same immediate removal applies.

Your rights

You can, at any time: - Export all of your data as a JSON file (Settings → Backup). - Delete your account and all associated data (Settings → Danger Zone). This is permanent. - Request a copy of, or the deletion of, any data we hold about you by contacting [SUPPORT EMAIL].

These rights apply to all users regardless of location, in line with data protection principles such as the EU's GDPR and California's CCPA/CPRA.

Age requirement

Anvil is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. [If you intend to support a younger audience, this section needs separate, dedicated legal review — COPPA in the US imposes specific requirements for under-13 users.]

Data retention & security

  • Data transmitted to our servers (invite emails, partner-sync fields) is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) only.
  • Data at rest is encrypted by our hosting/database provider.
  • We retain shared partner data only for as long as the partnership remains active, or until either party deletes their account or unlinks.
